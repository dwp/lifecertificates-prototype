If you get UK State Pension and live abroad, you can use this service to confirm your identity.

This helps us to continue paying your UK State Pension.

This service lets you:

  • confirm your identity
  • check or update your contact details

This usually takes 2 minutes.

Before you start

You will need:

  • a phone, tablet or computer with a camera

You also need one of these:

  • a UK biometric passport
  • a biometric passport issued by your country of residence
  • a national identity card issued by your country of residence
  • photo ID showing your address, such as a driver license

You can also complete a Life Certificate abd send it by post.

You cannot use this service if you are living in the UK.

The online service is also available in Welsh (ar gael yn Gymraeg).
