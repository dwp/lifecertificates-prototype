This prototype represents the team's current understanding of how overseas pension recipients can complete a proof of life process digitally and provide evidence required to maintain their State Pension.

The prototype explores how customers receive and respond to a Life Certificate request, verify their identity, provide supporting evidence and understand what happens next.

Users and roles

Primary users

  • Overseas pension recipients completing a proof of life journey.
  • Representatives acting on behalf of pension recipients.

Supporting roles

  • Life Certificate operations teams.
  • Service support agents.
  • Researchers.
  • Designers.
  • Policy and subject matter experts.

Current focus

This version focuses on reducing uncertainty in areas that require further validation before progressing the service design.

  • Whether customers can complete a proof of life journey digitally.
  • Whether customers can verify their identity and provide supporting evidence.
  • Whether known customers can review and confirm existing information with confidence.
  • How representatives, attorneys and other third parties may interact with the service.
  • What support is needed for customers who cannot complete the journey independently.

Measures of success

  • Customers understand what they need to do and why.
  • Customers can progress through the journey without unexpected blockers.
  • Customers can provide the information and evidence needed to complete proof of life.
  • Known customers can review and confirm existing information with confidence.
  • Customers understand confirmation and next-step messages.

Known gaps

  • Placeholder interactions are used for document scanning and proof of life.
  • Providing a Cert de Vie is not yet represented.
  • Identity verification success is not communicated.
  • Error states and recovery journeys remain incomplete.
  • Notifications and reminders are not yet prototyped.
  • Support agent and service manager journeys are not fully represented.

What's changed in this version

Added

  • Representative pathway because support for third-party journeys requires further exploration.
  • Additional guidance content because some stages of the journey required more support.

Updated

  • Evidence guidance because customers needed clearer information about what they need to provide.
  • Identity verification content because customers were unsure why verification was required.
  • Confirmation messaging because customers needed clearer information about outcomes and next steps.

Removed

  • Duplicate guidance content because it was no longer adding value to the journey.
