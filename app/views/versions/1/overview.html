This page shows how the main journey flows in version {{ version.number }} to address user needs.

User needs

  • As a customer, I need to send my life certificate, so that I can continue to receive pension payments.

Simplified journey flow

Common start

  1. Customer starts journey
  2. Scan ID

Then the journey takes one of two paths:

Path 1: digital verification

  1. Provide proof of life
  2. Share, review or change DWP-held information
  3. Provide proof of life again
    (if changing DWP-held info)
  4. Service processes immediately
  5. Customer sees processed confirmation

Path 2: doctor’s letter

  1. Provide contact details without seeing DWP-held information
  2. Submit proof of life
  3. Service schedules case review
  4. Customer sees review confirmation
  5. Support agent reviews case

Common start

From variation 1 (zero knowledge of the customer)

  • Before you start
  • Scan front of document
  • Check front of document
  • Scan back of document
  • Check back of document

Then the journey takes one of two paths:

Digital verification path

    Customers share their information with us

  • Verify identity
  • Provide email address
  • Provide mobile number
  • Verify mobile number
  • Choose preferred contact method
  • Check your answers

Variation 2

Customers can review and update the information we hold about them

  • Verify identity
  • Review pension payment bank details
  • Review email address
  • Review mobile number
  • Review preferred contact method
  • Check your answers

Variation 3

As variation 2 and customers can review their registered lasting power of attorney

  • Review registered lasting power of attorney
  • Remove a registered lasting power of attorney
  • Register a power of attorney
  • Check your answers

Journey end

  • Confirmation

Doctor's letter path

Customers provide a letter from their doctor

  • Enter doctor's details and upload letter
  • Enter doctor's contact information
  • Check your answers
  • Confirmation
