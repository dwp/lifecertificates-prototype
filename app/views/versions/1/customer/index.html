Customer journey

How a customer starts and provides a life certificate.

{% set variation1Path = baseUrl + "/zero-knowledge-proof-of-life/" %} {% set variation1Journey %}

No DWP-held information is shared with the customer.

  1. Get ready to start
  2. Provide a verifiable identity document
    1. Scan front of document
    2. Check front of document
    3. Scan back of document
    4. Check back of document
  3. Either verify identity/proof of life (preferred)
    1. Verify identity
  4. Or share life verification from a doctor
    1. Enter doctor's details and upload letter
    2. Enter doctor's contact information
  5. Provide contact details
    1. Provide email address
    2. Provide mobile number
    3. Verify mobile number
    4. Choose preferred contact method
  6. Check answers and send your life certificate
    1. Check your answers
    2. Confirmation

After proving their identity customers can review (and request changes to) information DWP holds about them.

  1. Get ready to start
  2. Provide a verifiable identity document
    1. Scan front of document
    2. Check front of document
    3. Scan back of document
    4. Check back of document
  3. Either verify identity/proof of life and then review and/or change known contact information (preferred)
    1. Verify identity

    then

    1. Review pension payment bank details
    2. Review email address
    3. Review mobile number
    4. Verify mobile number (if changed)
    5. Review preferred contact method

    and then

    1. Verify identity again
  4. Or share life verification from a doctor and provide up to date contact details
    1. Enter doctor's details and upload letter
    2. Enter doctor's contact information
  5. Check answers and send your life certificate
    1. Check your answers
    2. Confirmation
{{ govukButton({ text: "Start variation 2", href: variation2Path + "start", isStartButton: true }) }} {% endset %} {% set variation3Path = baseUrl + "/tell-us-about-lpa/" %} {% set variation3Journey %}

As variation 2 with the option to tell DWP about any granted power of attorney.

  1. Get ready to start
  2. Provide a verifiable identity document
    1. Scan front of document
    2. Check front of document
    3. Scan back of document
    4. Check back of document
  3. Either verify identity/proof of life and then review and/or change known contact information (preferred)
    1. Verify identity

    then

    1. Review pension payment bank details
    2. Review registered lasting power of attorney
      1. Remove a registered lasting power of attorney (optional)
      2. Register a power of attorney (optional, signpost to LPA guidance)
    3. Review email address
    4. Review mobile number
    5. Verify mobile number (if changed)
    6. Review preferred contact method

    and then

    1. Verify identity again
  4. Or share life verification from a doctor and provide up to date contact details
    1. Enter a doctor's details and upload a letter from them
    2. Enter a doctor's contact information
  5. Check answers and send your life certificate
    1. Check your answers
    2. Confirmation
